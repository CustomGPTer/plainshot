Privacy

Plainshot collects nothing. It has no internet access, so it could not send anything if it tried.

What the app does with your photos

  • It reads the photos and videos on your phone to show them in its gallery and to work out which are safe.
  • It cleans the photos you take through it (or all of them, with Always on), moves the clean copy to Internal storage › DCIM › Plainshot and deletes the original.
  • It keeps a small list on the phone of what each file was found to hold, so it does not have to read every photo again, and a list of what is in its 30-day bin.

All of that happens on the phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, adverts, analytics or crash reports.

Permissions

  • All files access. To clean photos in the Camera folder, move them, and show every album.
  • Usage access. To see when the camera app closes, so cleaning stops then and a video is not touched while it records. Plainshot looks only at which app is on screen while it is working; it keeps no record.
  • Notifications. To show that cleaning is running, with a Stop button.
  • Run at start-up. To start Always on again after the phone restarts, if you switched it on.

Plainshot does not have the internet permission. You can see the full list on the phone: Settings, Apps, Plainshot, Permissions.

Share safe

When you choose Share safe, a cleaned copy is made in the app's own scratch space and handed to the app you pick. What that app does with it is up to that app. Scratch copies are cleared after a day.
