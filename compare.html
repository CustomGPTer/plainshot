What Plainshot does not do

Plainshot does not change what the camera saw, and it cannot edit details, only take them out. RAW files are left alone. It needs Android 13 or later and is not on Google Play, so look at the release notes for new versions.

Where the others do more

Exif Cleaner's Pro version can change dates and places rather than just remove them, and both apps are on Google Play, so they update themselves.
