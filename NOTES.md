First public release.

- Tap Shoot and the phone's own camera opens. Each photo is cleaned a few seconds after you take it and moved to the Plainshot album.
- Location, date and time, phone model, camera settings, thumbnail, Samsung's extras and the motion clip are taken out, each with a switch to keep it. The picture itself is copied byte for byte.
- A gallery of every photo on the phone, with a Safe pill on the ones that hold nothing beyond what your settings allow.
- Make safe for photos already on the phone, Share safe in the share menu, and Always on for every photo the phone takes.
- A quick-settings tile, a home-screen shortcut and a 30-day bin.
- JPEG, HEIC, PNG and WebP photos; MP4 and MOV videos.
- No internet permission.

Download **Plainshot.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android 13 or newer.
Installing notes: https://customgpter.github.io/plainshot/#installing
