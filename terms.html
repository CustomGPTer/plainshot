What to know before relying on it

  • Plainshot deletes the original of each photo it cleans. That is its purpose. What was taken out cannot be got back.
  • It removes hidden information from files. It does not hide what is visible in a picture, and it cannot control what other apps or services do with a photo before it is cleaned or after you share it.
  • The Safe pill means Plainshot read the file and found nothing beyond what your settings allow. It is not a guarantee that a photo cannot be traced by other means.
  • Keep backups of photos that matter to you.
