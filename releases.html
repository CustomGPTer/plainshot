1.0 18 November 2026

First public release.

  • Tap Shoot and the phone's own camera opens. Each photo is cleaned a few seconds after you take it and moved to the Plainshot album.
  • Location, date and time, phone model, camera settings, thumbnail, Samsung's extras and the motion clip are taken out, each with a switch to keep it.
  • A gallery of every photo on the phone, with a Safe pill on the ones that are.
  • Make safe for photos already on the phone, and Share safe in the share menu.
  • Always on, a quick-settings tile, a home-screen shortcut and a 30-day bin.
  • JPEG, HEIC, PNG and WebP photos; MP4 and MOV videos.
  • No internet permission.

Plainshot cannot check for updates itself, because it has no internet access. Every version is on the GitHub releases page.

Plainshot is free. No adverts, no accounts, no paid version.
