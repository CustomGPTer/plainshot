Release notes

What changed in each version of Plainshot. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0 18 November 2026Latest

  • First public release. Tap Shoot and the phone's own camera opens. Each photo is cleaned a few seconds after you take it and moved to the Plainshot album.
  • Location, date and time, phone model, camera settings, thumbnail, Samsung's extras and the motion clip are taken out, each with a switch to keep it.
  • A gallery of every photo on the phone, with a Safe pill on the ones that are.
  • Make safe for photos already on the phone, and Share safe in the share menu.
  • Always on, a quick-settings tile, a home-screen shortcut and a 30-day bin.
  • JPEG, HEIC, PNG and WebP photos; MP4 and MOV videos.
  • No internet permission.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0 is called Plainshot.apk. It is 2.7 MB (2,708,042 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

34209aced56e389715cb63e31305a885f24ca85ae43bdef9ee2b650d200502db

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Plainshot.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
